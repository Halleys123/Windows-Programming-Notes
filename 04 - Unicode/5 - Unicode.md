If [[4 - Double Byte Character Set|DBCS]] was the messy, desperate hack to get Asian languages working on 8-bit systems **Unicode** is the clean, mathematically perfect reboot of the entire system of DBCS.

**Unicode is a universal mapping standard.** It is maintained by the Unicode Consortium, and its only job is to assign a unique, abstract ID number (called a "Code Point") to every single character in human existence
The C programming language as formalized by ANSI and inherently supports Unicode.

> Unicode was initially only meant to assist ASCII and by any chance replace it, but then it became mainstream in today's world.
